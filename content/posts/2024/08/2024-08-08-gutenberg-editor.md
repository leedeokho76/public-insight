# 워드프레스 구텐베르크 편집기 단축키

## Important Hotkey

- Show block to add: /
- Shift between cord editor and visual editor: Ctrl Shft Alt M
- Show Hotkey: Alt Shft H

## Text Form

- Bold: Ctrl B
- Italic: Ctrl I
- Under Line: Ctrl U
- Add Link to Selection: Ctrl K
- Show Hotkey: Alt Shift H

## Global

- Change Editor: Ctrl Shift Alt M
- Full Screen Mode: Ctrl Shift Alt F
- Show Block List: Shift Alt O
- Setting Sidebar on/off: Ctrl Shift ,
- Move Previous Part of Editor: Ctrl Shift Shift Alt P
- Move Next Part of Editor: Ctrl Shift Alt N
- Move near by Part of Editor: Alt F10

## Block

- Copy: Ctrl Shift D
- Eraser: Shift Alt Z
- Add New Block to Front of the Selection: Ctrl Alt T
- Add New Block to Behind of the Selection: Ctrl Alt Y
- Delete Block: del, backspace
- Move to Front of the Selection: Ctrl Shift Alt T
- Move to Behind of the Selection: Ctrl Shift Alt Y
- Add New Paragraph and then Change Block Type: /
